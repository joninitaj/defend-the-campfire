# Defend the Campfire

A Vampire Survivors-style arena game. Hold the camp against waves of ghouls. Keep the fire lit if you can. If it dies, you still fight — camp upgrades just stop.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages link on this repo.

For a local server:

```
npx serve
```

Then open the URL it prints.

## Controls

**Desktop (landscape)**

- **Enter** or click to start
- **WASD** / arrow keys move
- **Space** dash
- **P** pause
- **R** restart
- **F** fullscreen
- **1** **2** **3** pick an upgrade

**Mobile (portrait or landscape)**

- Tap to start
- On-screen joystick to move
- **DASH** to dash
- Tap cards and buttons for upgrades, pause, and ads

## Gameplay

Survive waves around the camp. Weapons fire on their own. After each wave, choose a weapon or passive. Every 3 waves, while the fire still burns, you also get a camp upgrade.

Hearts drop from some kills. Stand near a living fire to heal. A brute shows up every 5 waves. After wave 10 the run goes endless.

## Ads

CrazyGames HTML5 SDK v3. Rewarded video only, and only if you opt in:

- After death, watch an ad to respawn once per run, or restart without an ad
- During a fight, a 3× power-up offer can appear. Watch an ad for three extra picks, or skip

Locally you get a short preview overlay instead of a live ad.
